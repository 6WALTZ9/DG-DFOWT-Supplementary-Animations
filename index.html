Reading the animations

Each animation compares five cases in an x–z section through the rotor center. Colors show the normalized streamwise velocity U/Uref. Black segments indicate the moving rotor plane, the gray dash-dotted line marks the undisturbed hub height, and the white dashed line marks the modeled wake centerline.

All four videos use the same physical-time mapping: approximately 0–296.67 seconds of physical time is shown in 20 seconds of playback at 24 frames per second. Here St = fD/Uref, where f is the platform-motion frequency, D is the rotor diameter, and Uref is the reference wind speed.

These animations visualize analytical model outputs; they are not CFD-LES results or independent validation data.
